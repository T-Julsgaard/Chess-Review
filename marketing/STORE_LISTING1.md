Completely free. No paywalls, no subscriptions, no paid tiers.

Turn any Chess.com or Lichess game into a full chess review in one click.

Chess Review is powered by Stockfish running locally in your browser, giving you detailed game analysis without requiring an account or payment.

KEY FEATURES
• Accuracy estimates for both players and move-by-move classifications.
• Move-by-move classifications — from Brilliant to Blunder.
• Best-move arrows — instantly see the engine's recommended continuation.
• Evaluation graph — follow how the advantage changed throughout the game.
• Estimated performance rating — see how strongly each player performed.
• Automatic opening detection — identifies your opening using a built-in opening book.
• Interactive analysis board — explore your own variations with live Stockfish evaluation.

HOW IT WORKS
Open a game on Chess.com or Lichess and start a review. Chess Review runs Stockfish directly in your browser and analyzes the game locally on your device.

Once complete, you get a full move-by-move review with classifications, accuracy scores, evaluations, best moves, performance ratings, and opening information.
