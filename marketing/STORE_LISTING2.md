## Short description

Free, open-source analysis for your chess games.

## Overview

Understand your games and explore better moves with Chess Review, an independent,
open-source extension powered by Stockfish running locally in your browser.

Review games from Chess.com or Lichess, or paste a game URL or PGN.
Chess Review is intended for reviewing games after play.

- Accuracy estimates for both players and move-by-move classifications.
- Evaluation graphs, best-move arrows, and an estimated performance rating.
- Offline opening detection and interactive variation exploration.
- Customizable boards, piece sets, sounds, and animated coaches.
- Stockfish 18 NNUE by default, with Stockfish 19 Lite available in Settings.

Scores and performance ratings are estimates calculated using the extension's
scoring rules. They can differ from other review tools and are not official
Chess.com or Lichess ratings.

Designed for post-game review and study. Do not use engine assistance during an
ongoing game.

Analysis runs on your device. Public game lookup requests go directly to the chess
platforms. There is no developer backend, analytics, or advertising.

Chess Review is independent and unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or sponsored by Chess.com or Lichess.

## Privacy summary

Analysis runs locally. Public game lookup requests, including usernames or game
identifiers, go directly to Chess.com or Lichess. The extension reads game-related
information from those pages and stores preferences, fetched public game data,
and analysis locally. It has no developer backend, analytics, or advertising.

Choosing Share game copies a link containing encoded, unencrypted PGN and game
metadata to your clipboard. Sharing it is your choice; anyone with the complete
link can read its contents. The extension does not upload it to a developer server.
