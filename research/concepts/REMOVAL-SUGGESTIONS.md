# Suggestions only: retain teaching scope, avoid redundant detector research

No nonduplicate entry has been removed. I do not see a basis to promise that deleting a unique chess idea would preserve all coaching quality. The safer change is to move tasks to the right kind of implementation, while retaining their educational content. All IDs remain in the catalog and proposed queue. Phase 6 is only a proposal; other suggestions below remain in their assigned phases because they may need real explanatory research.

| Original IDs | Suggestion | Why / what must remain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C0847–C0849, C1030 | Remove as separate new move-category classifiers; reuse existing categories. | The extension already presents Blunder, Mistake and Inaccuracy (analysis.js, quality labels/classification). Keep teaching why a move failed through specific motifs and alternatives. Do not replace the current classification thresholds in this review. |
| C0985–C0990, C0995–C1006 | Move notation, formats and annotation symbols to glossary/parser/UI work. | SAN is a specific algebraic notation format, not a confirmed duplicate. Preserve explanations, serialization and accessibility; do not run a new scientific move detector for each symbol. |
| C1012–C1017, C1020 | Move engine vocabulary and diagnostic quantities to documentation/UI. | Engine, centipawn, depth, nodes and Multi-PV are support concepts. Preserve the actual analysis capabilities. Tablebases, alternative moves, mate proofs and evaluation swings can be essential inputs or outputs; retain them. |
| C1047–C1058 | Defer competition formats, standings and titles from the board-concept detector program. | They are contextual/reference concepts, not identifiable from a board move. Keep lessons/tooltips if desired. Rating and time-control context may influence coaching and should not be discarded with them. |
| C0835–C0846 | Move study activities to the coach feature/lesson backlog. | Post-game analysis, self-analysis, pattern/tactical/calculation/endgame training, opening study, model games and guess-the-move are valuable functions; each is not a new board detector. |
| C0794–C0795, C0832, C0870, C0873–C0876 | Remove claims to detect a player's hidden mental state from moves alone. | Retain intuition/judgment/psychology lessons and observable errors. A missed resource does not prove overconfidence, tunnel vision, hope chess or the point at which calculation stopped. User input could support a different claim later. |
| C0018–C0019, C0814–C0820, C0871–C0872, C1033–C1040 | Put rule-of-conduct, resignation and clock context behind suitable input availability. | Touch-move needs interaction evidence; resignation needs game termination metadata; time management needs clocks, not a FEN. Retain teaching and defer automatic assertions until the data exists. Increment/delay exact repeats were merged only as duplicate rows, with both contexts retained. |
| C0119–C0122, C0789–C0796 | Treat thinking skills as teaching objectives rather than observed mental actions. | Visualization, board vision, pattern recognition and calculation can shape exercises/explanations. A board position alone does not establish what the player saw or thought. Calculation trees and useful candidate explanations remain research work. |

Keep the broad chess substance: rare mates, endgame methods, fortress/zugzwang, good-versus-bad pieces, prophylaxis, sacrifice, compensation, initiative, exchanges and planning questions. Their difficulty does not make them dispensable. Combine related concepts into studies and explanations without pretending they are identical.

The recommendation is a smaller **new-detector** program with a full teaching catalog, not a smaller ambition for the coach. Approval of these proposals would still need a concrete mapping to runtime, UI, glossary or lessons; none has been implemented here.
