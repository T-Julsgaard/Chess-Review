# Definition sources

2026-10-07. Definitions/metadata only; no source positions, boards, games,
FENs, sequences or evaluations copied into authored fixtures.

- [Chess.com official terminology: Overloading](https://www.chess.com/terms/overloading-chess): a defensive piece has more responsibilities than it can fulfill. E049 requires actual legal recapture duties and a conditional material consequence, not geometric duty counts alone.
- [FIDE Laws, 2023](https://rcc.fide.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Laws_of_Chess-2023.pdf): king-safety constraints distinguish attack geometry from legal capture duties.
- Original supplied list: Overloading, Overworked defender, Deflection combination. Existing frozen mating deflections are different certificates; E049 adds a finite material capture/recapture/capture deflection witness.

Nominal material is explicitly p1/n3/b3/r5/q9/k0. The certificate proves a
finite horizon on conditional acceptance; it does not prove optimal play,
forced acceptance, permanent gain or a good overall move.
