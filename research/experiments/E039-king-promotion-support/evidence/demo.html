Forks need a capture witness against every legal reply, with positive nominal material after immediate counterreplies. This finite check does not establish the best move or a winning position. Praise and strategic advice await broader validation.

Earlier endgame and trade examples · Tactical and structural examples · Inherited cases are checked and fingerprinted in results.json.
