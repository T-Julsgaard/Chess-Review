# Sources and scope

Reviewed 2026-10-07: [FIDE Laws of Chess](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023),
articles 1–3, for initial setup, legal movement and mandatory check response.
Definitions only; no source games or diagram positions are evaluated.

Development labels use explicit operational history gates in plan.md. Tempo
here means development accompanied by check, not net time or strategic gain.
Early/repeated labels state counts; they do not recommend against a move.
All evaluated sequences are authored synthetic fixtures.
