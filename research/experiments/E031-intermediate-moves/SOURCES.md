# Definition sources

Reviewed 2026-10-07. [Chess.com: Zwischenzug](https://www.chess.com/terms/zwischenzug-chess)
defines an in-between move that interrupts an expected continuation with a
forcing threat. Here the narrow objective context is a verified preceding
capture and legal recapture; checks/other captures retain a finite all-defense
recapture proof, or the move actually mates. Intention and necessity are not
inferred. Only definition metadata read; no source moves, diagrams or game
positions imported. All evaluated fixtures are authored synthetic controls.
