## Exact candidate, claim and reason

E008's CP model improves human outcome/choice likelihood in development, while
E010 shows that the fitted choice distributions are insufficiently stable.
This experiment isolates the **unchanged scalar outcome curve**, whose focal
root points passed both operational tolerances. It does not rehabilitate the
choice model or convert a failed experiment into a pass. No temperature, choice
probabilities, display transform or category labels are assessed/promoted here.

Candidate sigmoid(0.22349935786891822*cp/100), fixed comparator
sigmoid(0.368208*cp/100). Copy exact curve objects from the registered E010 freeze,
register/commit the new curve-only freeze and code before collecting. No new
fit, calibration, intercept, covariate, clipping search or alternative candidate.
Also compare with uninformative expected points0.5; this is a fixed simple
comparator, not fitted using confirmation data.

Bounded claim: improved expected **human game points** prediction at the declared
CP-scored roots, under this cohort and exact SF19 search pipeline. Fractional
Bernoulli loss/Brier assess expected points, not three-class W/D/L probabilities
or causal move values. No universal/population, SF18, latent-strength, rating,
game accuracy percentage, brilliant-move or instructional claim.
