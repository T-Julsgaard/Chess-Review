# Definition sources

Reviewed 2026-10-07. Definition metadata only; no source diagrams, moves,
games or examples imported. All evaluated positions are authored synthetic
mechanics, with no real-game or educational precision claim.

- [Chess.com: Sacrifice](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-sacrifice): giving
  material for another advantage. Here that advantage is independently proven
  forced mate, including every legal acceptance and decline.
- [Chess.com: Chess tactics](https://www.chess.com/article/view/chess-tactics?page=2):
  exchange sacrifice gives rook for a minor; clearance sacrifice vacates a
  square or line. Here exchange requires an actual minor captured before a
  legally available rook loss; clearance requires a newly opened checking ray.

Offers may be declined; labels say offers, not that the piece was already lost
or that the sacrifice was necessary. Long-term compensation remains outside scope.
