# Definition metadata

Read 2026-10-07. No source boards, games, FENs, sequences or evaluations used.

- Original supplied list: Square clearance means vacating a square another piece needs; Temporary sacrifice means material is regained shortly afterward; Clearance combination.
- [Chess.com official tactics definitions](https://www.chess.com/article/view/chess-tactics?page=2): clearance can concern a square or a line. E050 specifically certifies the vacated square as another unit's mating destination against all legal replies, without asserting intent.
- [Chess.com official sacrifice terminology](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-sacrifice): material offers can have tactical compensation. E050 requires negative actual accepted material change followed by complete finite immediate material restoration; a mate alone is not a material-recovery witness.
- [FIDE Laws, 2023](https://rcc.fide.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Laws_of_Chess-2023.pdf): legal moves, captures, en passant and king-safety constraints.

Material conventions are stated explicitly. Conditional acceptance, immediate
recovery and one ensuing enemy response are the proof scope, not forced
acceptance, long-term value or objective move quality.
