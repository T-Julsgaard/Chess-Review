No external games, positions or diagrams acquired. Inputs are locally authored
synthetic development positions and retained E079 foundation/terminal guards.
All entry points open D001 through the guarded loader before input access;
synthetic exposure is development mechanics, not independent game precision.

Retained source modules:

- E079 center.mjs: exact opt-in disabled parent API/event/selection baseline.
- E027 defense.mjs turnBoard: explicit hypothetical opponent-turn snapshot,
  clearing en-passant on a turn change; no actual legal-pass/history claim.

- E029 replay.mjs replayQuery: independent complete branch/leaf semantics.
- E024 validateHistory and E020 legalPosition: actual full history/FEN legality.
- E079 pool.mjs/saved.mjs: lossless complete support-tree storage and independent
  decoder, version/hash/reference validation; semantic replay still required.

New replayer imports no new detector or source solver. It separately enumerates
all board squares, cover offsets, scalar rays and finite one-ply transitions;
supplied proof trees must also pass frozen E029 semantic replay. Default disabled
remains exact E079, new exhaustion atomic, parent warning priorities preserved.

C0226 and C0400 candidate coverage is bounded protection by an actual cover
pawn advance that blocks a complete prior hypothetical mating capture and
leaves no actual opponent mate in one. It does not discharge enduring king
safety, all pawn cover/king-shelter meanings, shield destruction or quality.
Comments name the concept and witnessed effect in at most 24 words.
