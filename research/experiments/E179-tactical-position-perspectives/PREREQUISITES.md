# Evaluation prerequisites retained

2026-10-10. C0569 static evaluation, C0571 equal position, C0572 slight advantage
and C0573 clear advantage remain without a candidate in this batch. Specify
their intended target/rubric and validate against suitable independent evidence
or complete relevant outcomes. A hand-chosen nominal-material/CP threshold is
not this evidence. Failure to find short mate is not equality or a draw; one
compared bad move does not establish a losing root when alternatives remain.

E179's winning/losing scopes concern the exact actual after-move frame, explicit
perspective and complete positive finite mate certificate. They do not grade
arbitrary static positions or define small/clear advantages. Longer nonmating
winning positions and all broader strategic definitions remain required work.
No numerical-goal resumption or new data acquisition is authorized by this note.
