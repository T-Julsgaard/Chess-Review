Coach concepts · pawn breaks

Pawn breaks challenge a ram or release/escape an actual blocker. Every legal reply retains the named pawn and supplies the required legal next-turn capture or advance, unless a challenged blocker vacates. No advantage or permanent-release claim. Full proofs are retained in results.json. Authored positions; no extension integration.

Forks need a capture witness against every legal reply, with positive nominal material after immediate counterreplies. This finite check does not establish the best move or a winning position. Praise and strategic advice await broader validation.
