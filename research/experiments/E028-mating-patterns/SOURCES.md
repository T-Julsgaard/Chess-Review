# E028 definition sources

Reviewed 2026-10-07. Definitions only; no game moves, puzzles, diagrams or
historical positions are imported or evaluated. All fixtures are authored
synthetic geometry. No source claim about teaching benefit is adopted.

- [Chess.com: Arabian mate](https://www.chess.com/terms/arabian-mate-chess):
  adjacent rook checks; knight protects it and controls a flight square.
- [Chess.com: Anastasia's mate](https://www.chess.com/terms/anastasias-mate-chess):
  edge-file rook/queen check with knight controlling inward diagonal flights.
- [Chess.com: Boden's mate](https://www.chess.com/terms/bodens-mate-chess):
  bishop pair uses crossing diagonals and self-blocked escapes.
- [Chess.com: checkmate patterns](https://www.chess.com/terms/checkmate-chess):
  ladder/rook roller uses parallel rook barriers; epaulette has self-blocked
  shoulders; Opera uses rook/bishop cooperation; Morphy uses a bishop check,
  rook restriction and self-blocked flight. Exact conservative templates below
  are experimental subsets, not an exhaustive definition of each named mate.
- [Chess.com: epaulette mate article](https://www.chess.com/article/view/mating-patterns-2-epaulette-mate):
  queen two ranks from king, with enemy pieces on both same-rank shoulders.
- [GM Mauricio Flores Rios: dovetail mate](https://chessmind.ai/chess-terms/dovetail-mate):
  protected diagonal-adjacent queen; two uncovered adjacent squares self-blocked.
  Its family comparison defines swallow's-tail as protected orthogonal-adjacent
  queen with the two rear-diagonal flights self-blocked.

No direct quotations. Labels whose necessary geometry/history is not settled
here (e.g. Legal, Hook, Damiano/Lolli distinction and combination history) remain
outside E028. Generic piece-pair, pawn-supported, discovered/double-check and
promotion mates name directly verifiable roles in an actual terminal position.
