Rubric and review instructions

Consequence: sound/equivalent preserves prospects; small concession is modest avoidable worsening; clear mistake is a meaningful tactical or lasting disadvantage; major mistake is likely to change the practical result. Explain a better alternative for mistake judgments. A bad position alone does not imply a bad move.

Exceptional idea: ordinary, notable idea, brilliant candidate, or unresolved. A brilliant candidate needs a concrete non-obvious, sound-looking idea worth teaching. Material offers, routine recaptures, forced moves and flashy unsound tactics are insufficient. Explain compensation or the unique resource. Human perception does not establish tactical soundness.

Choose confidence and instructional usefulness separately. State ambiguity or missing context. Review independently before opening the selection key or discussing cases. One reviewer supplies individual evidence; this pack does not certify extension labels.
