# Reviewer rubric v1

Assess the move in its actual position. Use your own analysis and explain the
concrete consequence or idea. You may leave a case unresolved. Do not try to
guess an engine label or another site's classification.

## Decision consequence

- **Sound / equivalent:** a defensible move preserving the position's prospects;
  includes several equally sensible choices and forced solutions.
- **Small concession:** an avoidable but modest worsening; explain what was lost.
- **Clear mistake:** a meaningful avoidable worsening, such as a serious tactical
  miss or an enduring disadvantage with no adequate compensation.
- **Major mistake:** an avoidable change likely to alter the game's practical
  result, such as losing decisive material or allowing a forced finish.
- **Unresolved:** insufficient analysis or a position outside your confidence.

These are qualitative instructional judgments, not a mapping to fixed CP/Elo
bands. A bad final position alone does not establish a bad move. State the better
alternative when making a mistake judgment, and distinguish difficulty from
consequence. No penalty for abstention.

## Exceptional-move perception

Choose **ordinary**, **notable idea**, **brilliant candidate**, or **unresolved**.
A brilliant candidate should have a specific, non-obvious and sound-looking
idea worth explaining, beyond simply being best. An offer of material alone,
a routine recapture, a forced move or a flashy but unsound tactic is insufficient.
Record whether you see a material investment, an unusually important unique
resource, a quiet idea or another concrete property. Human perception does not
prove tactical soundness; engine/history checks will assess those separately.

## Confidence and usefulness

Choose confidence **low / medium / high** and instructional usefulness
**low / medium / high**. Give a short reason and optionally a suggested line.
For exceptional judgments, explain the compensation/idea and why it was hard
to see. For mistakes, explain the missed resource or consequence. Flag ambiguity,
missing context or rubric problems. Save/export partial work whenever convenient.

Review independently before discussing cases or opening the private selection
key. Later revisions must preserve the original review and state their reason.
This first pack develops the rubric; it does not certify any extension label.
