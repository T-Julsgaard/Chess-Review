# Definitions and scope

Reviewed 2026-10-07: Chess.com's own [decoy definition](https://www.chess.com/terms/decoy-chess)
distinguishes drawing an enemy onto a vulnerable square from diverting it from
a defensive task; [deflection](https://www.chess.com/terms/deflection-chess)
provides the related duty-removal term. An authored coach's
[decoy tutorial](https://www.chess.com/blog/attilaturzo/tactics-decoy) identifies
king attraction as the king-specific form. Definition metadata only; no source
games, positions, diagrams or puzzles imported.

The experimental rules are stricter than descriptive geometry: complete
all-defense mate proof plus explicit legal acceptance and immediate mate;
deflection also proves a legal previous defender capture; a self-blocking role
has an explicitly artificial removed-blocker legal king escape; king attraction
has the same before move legally available but not mate. No general intentions
or necessary-acceptance claims are inferred.
