# Definitions and scope

Reviewed 2026-10-07: Chess.com's own [sacrifice definition](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-sacrifice)
describes giving up material to pursue another goal; its [defense definition](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-defense)
includes preventing checkmate and distinguishes counterattacking possibilities.
Definition metadata only: no source game, diagram, puzzle or evaluation imported.

Here the concrete defensive goal is avoiding mate in one. Every legal alternative
fails that goal and the played move has a complete safety proof. A counterattack
checks the enemy king. A sacrifice offer also has an explicit legal acceptance
with negative nominal balance through every immediate response. It does not
claim acceptance is forced or that longer-term compensation has been established.
