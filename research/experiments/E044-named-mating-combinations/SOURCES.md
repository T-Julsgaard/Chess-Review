# Definitions and scope

The original user list explicitly relates the h7/h2 rook sacrifice to a castled
king. Both h/g coordinate profiles therefore require actual legal kingside
castling history and current home-rank kingside king placement, as well as
checking rook offer and a complete bounded mate proof. The c3/c6 exchange
profile establishes actual rook-for-minor offer and forced mate, without
inferring a Sicilian opening or strategic suitability.

Frozen E020/E028 mate-pattern definitions and E030 sacrifice/mate certificates
are reused. Smothered combination requires every legal defense to admit the
strict own-unit-surrounded knight mate. Back-rank tactic requires actual
rook/queen mate on the home rank behind at least two adjacent own pawns.
Combination/Tactic labels identify the certified multi-idea mating subset,
not general calculation skill. All evaluated positions/history are authored
synthetic fixtures; no external game, diagram or engine evaluation imported.
