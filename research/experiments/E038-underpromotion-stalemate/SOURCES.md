# Sources and verification scope

[FIDE Laws of Chess](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023), articles 3.7.3
and 5.2.1, define promotion choice and stalemate. Definitions were reviewed in
the prior draw-history research; no source game or diagram is imported.
Actual legal positions and counterfactual same-square promotions are verified
using maintained chess.js and independent certificate replay.

All positions are authored synthetic fixtures. A conditional stalemate offer
is a legal reply witness, not a claim that the opponent accepts or cannot win.
