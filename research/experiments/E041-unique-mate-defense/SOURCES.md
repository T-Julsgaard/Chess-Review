# Proof scope

The supplied list defines Only move as necessary to maintain the position and
Forced move as compelled by threats. This experiment tests the concrete
necessity of avoiding immediate checkmate: every legal alternative admits mate
in one, while the played move does not. It does not infer intentions or longer
term safety. Frozen E029 legal-move mate queries and independent replay supply
the positive and negative certificates. Inputs are authored/exposed synthetic
positions; no external source games, diagrams or evaluations are imported.
