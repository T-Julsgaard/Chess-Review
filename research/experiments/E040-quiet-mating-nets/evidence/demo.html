Coach concepts · quiet mating nets

A quiet mating net requires mate on the next own move against every legal reply. Checking, capturing, longer-only and exhausted cases refuse this label. Full proofs are losslessly retained in certificates.json.gz. Authored positions; no extension integration.

Forks need a capture witness against every legal reply, with positive nominal material after immediate counterreplies. This finite check does not establish the best move or a winning position. Praise and strategic advice await broader validation.
