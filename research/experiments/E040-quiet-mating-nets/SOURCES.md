# Definition and proof scope

The original list contains mating net, mating attack, forcing moves and quiet
tactical move. This experiment defines a conservative operational subset:
a nonchecking, noncapturing move followed by mate against every legal defense
on the next own move. It reuses frozen E029's exhaustive legal-move mate proof
and independent replayer, rather than assuming safety from king geometry.

No external game, diagram or engine score supplies evaluated input. All cases
are authored synthetic positions or previously exposed synthetic fixtures.
The broad Quiet move entry's non-forcing wording is not established by a
forced mating threat and remains partial. Threat creation and best-move claims
are deliberately outside this certificate.
