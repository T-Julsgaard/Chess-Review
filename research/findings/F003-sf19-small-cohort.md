# F003: two small SF19 outcome/choice studies did not establish adoption

2026-10-05; accuracy outcome/choice models; original hypotheses inconclusive;
evidence retrospective development and previously consumed tests.

[E003](../experiments/E003-sf19-archive-replay/RESULT.md) reconstructed both
archived models and complete reports exactly from retained raw searches.
The full-curve candidate improved test choice likelihood by 0.352449 nats per
decision, with exploratory interval favoring it, but its outcome log-loss
difference remained unresolved. A later choice-only candidate's test choice
difference was -0.059080 with interval -0.500243 to +0.472324, failing its gate.
Each test contained twenty-five games/choices; they are different consumed sets.

The narrow choice result does not validate the displayed accuracy transform,
arithmetic aggregation, human categories or ratings. The later choice-only
result does not rule out useful effects on a larger/fresh population. Legacy
metadata claiming four choices per training game was inaccurate: these studies
used one choice in each of eighty training games; exact outputs preserve the
old text solely for auditability.

Decision: no promotion of these recipes. Use this finding to avoid repeating
the same small-data study as new evidence. A retry requires a revised hypothesis,
adequate sample-size reasoning and genuinely fresh decisive observations; report
outcome and choice claims separately before assessing any display percentage.
